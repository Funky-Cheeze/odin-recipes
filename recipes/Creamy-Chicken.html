Creamy Chicken and Rice Recipe

It's time to make your first easy meal! Making this meal is super easy and fun. It will easily make a tasty dinner for the whole family. Make sure you tell all your loved ones about this recipe and leave a comment on how yours turned out!

Ingredients

  • chicken breast/thighs
  • jasmine rice
  • chicken broth
  • garlic
  • heavy whipping cream

Steps

  1. Prepare chicken with desired seasonings
  2. Pan fry with oil until cooked through (2-5 minutes on each side for thighs)
  3. Combine rice, chicken broth and garlic in pot
  4. Bring to boil and simmer for 18-20 minutes covered
  5. Turn off heat and fluff rice. Add heavy cream and cover for 5 minutes
