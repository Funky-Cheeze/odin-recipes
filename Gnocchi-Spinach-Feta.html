Gnocchi Spinach with Feta Cheese

These skillet gnocchi with spinach and feta are a comforting lunch or dinner that's ready in less than 30 minutes. Golden on the outside and tender inside, the pan-fried gnocchi are tossed with spinach, feta, s un-dried tomatoes, and other flavor-packed ingredients for an easy, Mediterranean-inspired meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package gnocchi
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoon sun-dried tomato oil
  • 1/2 cup white wine, or reserved pasta water
  • 1/4 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil
  • 2 tablespoons sliced Kalamata olives
  • 1 teaspoon granulated garlic
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 4 cups fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese, sheep’s milk if available

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add gnocchi, and cook according to package directions, typically 2 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon to a paper towel to drain.
  2. Melt butter and sun-dried tomato oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add gnocchi and fry for 3 to 5 minutes, turning regularly. Add wine or reserved pasta water, sun-dried tomatoes, olives, garlic granules, salt, and pepper, and cook for 2 additional minutes.
  3. Stir in spinach, and cook until it wilts, 1 to 2 minutes. Gently fold in feta cheese, and serve.
